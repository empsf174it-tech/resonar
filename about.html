About Us

Engineering the Sound of the Future.

A collective of audio engineers, podcasters, and data analysts building a more honest way to choose studio gear.

Our Story

Resonar was born out of frustration. In a market flooded with exaggerated marketing claims and generic USB microphones, we wanted to create a haven for serious creators.

We are a collective of audio engineers, podcasters, and data analysts. We believe that choosing studio gear should be an objective, measurable process. That's why we build deep analytics and 360° visualization tools directly into our shopping experience.

Our Testing Methodology

Independence and objectivity are our core tenets.

1. The A-B Studio: Every microphone and interface is tested in our acoustically treated studio (RT60 < 0.2s). We do not rely on manufacturer spec sheets.

2. Frequency Response: We measure the exact frequency response using a calibrated sweep generator, ensuring that the graphs we provide are accurate representations of what you will hear.

3. The Podcast Stress Test: Clinical data is not enough. We test gear with real human voices—handling plosives, sibilance, and proximity effect—to ensure it performs in real-world podcasting scenarios.

Our Values

Objectivity

We don't do hype. If a microphone has a harsh 8kHz peak, we will show you exactly where it is on the frequency graph.

Creator First

From our analytics dashboard to our instant warranty registration, every tool we build is designed to save creators time.

Transparency

We list the pros and the cons. We tell you who should buy a product, and more importantly, who should skip it.
