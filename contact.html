FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions our studio team hears most.

How long does shipping take?

Every order ships free with 2-day delivery within the continental US. You'll get a tracking link by email as soon as your gear leaves the studio.

What is your return policy?

You have 30 days from delivery to return any item in its original condition for a full refund. Just reach out to support and we'll send a prepaid label.

How do I register my warranty?

Head to the Warranty page, enter your product's serial number, and it will appear in your dashboard instantly. All products include a 2-Year Studio Warranty.

Can I book an in-person demo?

Yes. Our Williamsburg listening room is open Mon–Fri, 9am – 6pm EST. Send us a message with the "Gear Advice" topic and we'll schedule a time.

Can you help me choose the right gear?

Absolutely. Tell us about your space and use case in the form above, or try our Audio Guides and Compare tool to narrow it down.
